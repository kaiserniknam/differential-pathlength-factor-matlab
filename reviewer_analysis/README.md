# Reviewer and diagnostic analyses

- `Rev_01.m` maps the approximate diffusion-theory validity boundary.
- `Rev_02.m` compares one-million- and ten-million-photon simulations.
- `Rev_03.m` examines mean-pathlength and slope-based DPF dependence.
- `Rev_04.m` visualizes optical density and photon exit distributions.

These scripts are retained separately from the primary paper workflow. Their
historical data paths will be refactored after the corresponding datasets are
provided.
